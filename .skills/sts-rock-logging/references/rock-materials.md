# Rock Materials

Capture rock type, secondary lithology, colour, grain size, fabric, lamination, bedding/foliation, interbedding, alteration, notes and confidence.

Material interval boundaries may differ from weathering and strength boundaries. Preserve each source boundary and render aligned tracks rather than forcing one combined row.

Do not infer rockhead from start of coring or first recovered core. Do not turn a soil-classified extremely weathered interval into competent rock for layout convenience.
