# Anti-AI Patterns

Reject purple/blue gradients, gradient headings, glassmorphism, decorative glow, nested cards, oversized welcome banners, repeated KPI-card grids, rounded icon tiles, sparkle motifs, meaningless charts, excessive pills/shadows, generic AI language, and huge whitespace that harms field density.

Reject generic SaaS dashboard composition. Use depth-aligned records, professional tables, compact action strips, explicit selected states and semantic validation.

Do not use a coloured side stripe as decoration. Do not pair wide soft shadows with bordered cards. Do not use decorative animation or hover-only critical controls.
