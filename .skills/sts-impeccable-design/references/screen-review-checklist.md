# Screen Review Checklist

- Does the first viewport identify project, borehole and current task?
- Are current depth/material/run, save/sync and validation visible where needed?
- Is one primary command obvious without ten equal buttons?
- Are selected, focus, disabled, loading, error and empty states complete?
- Are tables scannable with stable dimensions and sticky headers?
- Does phone use a focused workflow rather than clipped desktop layout?
- Are touch targets at least 44 px and keyboard paths intact?
- Is text contrast AA, non-overlapping and not truncated?
- Are cards non-nested, elevation restrained and STS green functional?
- Did desktop, tablet and phone screenshots and detector checks pass?
