# Defect Codes

Point families include joint, bedding parting, sheared seam, clay seam, crushed seam, infilled seam, natural break, drilling break and mechanical break.

Project-approved compact codes may include BP, JT, CS, SS, DB, MB and NB. Keep the structured type plus the display code.

Do not treat DB/MB as natural defects or include them in RQD/fracture spacing. Do not use JS for a group unless it is a consistent structural family.
