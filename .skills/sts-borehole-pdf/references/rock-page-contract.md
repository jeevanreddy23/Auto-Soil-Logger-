# Rock Page Contract

Required tracks include method/core run, depth/RL, graphic log, material, weathering, strength, defects/zones, TCR, RQD and fracture spacing as supported by the source.

- Show every defect; do not merge or summarize away records.
- Keep drilling/mechanical breaks visible and excluded from natural statistics.
- Draw recovery values once per value change.
- Render strength as a measured marker/track.
- Show termination and continuation consistently with soil sheets.
