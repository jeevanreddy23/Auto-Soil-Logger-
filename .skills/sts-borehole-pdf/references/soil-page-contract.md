# Soil Page Contract

Required first-page signals: STS identity, Borehole Log title, borehole ID, project/client/site metadata, drilling/location metadata, dates and review fields.

Depth-aligned tracks include groundwater, samples/tests, depth, graphic log, material description, classification symbol, consistency/relative density and moisture condition.

Render:

- Every interval boundary at source depth.
- Depth-specific subordinate notes at their point/range.
- Complete and partial/refusal SPT notation.
- Soil-to-rock continuation wording and termination.
- Notes legend and sheet number.
