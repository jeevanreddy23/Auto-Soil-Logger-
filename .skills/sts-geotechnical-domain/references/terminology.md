# Terminology

- Project: delivery and reporting metadata shared by boreholes.
- Borehole: location, setup, termination and requirement flags.
- Soil interval: from/to material observation; it is not an SPT or sample record.
- Rock material interval: lithology description; its boundaries may differ from weathering/strength.
- Core run: drilled/recovered interval used for TCR and RQD calculations.
- Point defect: one discontinuity at a depth; multiple records may share a depth.
- Defect zone: from/to interval such as CZ, FZ, SZ, XWZ, XWS or core loss.
- SPT: structured penetration test with blows and actual penetration.
- Sample: canonical collected/proposed specimen record, unique within the project.
- Groundwater observation: explicit state/time/depth, never inferred from blank input.
- Rockhead, start of coring, first recovery and first competent rock are distinct transitions.
- Drilling break is not a natural defect and is excluded from RQD/fracture statistics.
