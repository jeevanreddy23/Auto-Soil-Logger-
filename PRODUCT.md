# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

STS GeoFlow serves geotechnical engineers, engineering geologists, field technicians, drillers, reviewers, and project managers. Primary users capture and amend borehole observations beside drilling rigs on phones and tablets, often in direct sunlight and with intermittent connectivity. Reviewers and project managers work mainly on desktop, where they reconcile the complete depth record and issue controlled deliverables.

## Product Purpose

STS GeoFlow carries engineering observations from field capture to a professional, reproducible borehole-log PDF without losing depth, provenance, manual interpretation, or review state. Success means an existing project can be reopened, amended, validated, and regenerated while preserving its source record and issued history.

## Positioning

One canonical depth record connects fast field logging, engineering review, and OpenGround-quality STS deliverables.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, and dependable. The product should feel industrial and familiar to engineers: calm enough for review, dense enough for field work, and unmistakably STS without becoming decorative.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS dashboard, marketing page, AI chatbot, cryptocurrency product, or student project. Reject purple-blue gradients, glass surfaces, nested cards, oversized welcome areas, decorative animation, excessive pills and shadows, and generic "AI-powered" language.

## Design Principles

- Preserve engineering truth before simplifying interaction.
- Keep depth, current borehole, save state, sync state, and validation visible.
- Make repeated field entry fast while keeping detailed attributes reachable.
- Explain blockers in the context of the affected record and depth.
- Treat the PDF as a controlled view of the canonical source record, never a separate data model.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard operation, focus visibility, and semantic labels. Support 44 px minimum touch targets in field-facing phone views, do not rely on color alone, respect reduced motion, use tabular numerals for depths and calculations, and avoid hover-only controls.
